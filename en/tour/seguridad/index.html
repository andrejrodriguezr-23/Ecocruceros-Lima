A life vest for everyone

Safety

Your safety comes first

30 years of experience means clear protocols and the right gear for the whole family.

Safety equipment

Every passenger travels with a life vest. We also carry extra vests for those who want to swim with the sea lions, plus wetsuits (limited stock) in the colder season.

No swimming ability required

You can enter the sea wearing a life vest. Our crew stays with you throughout the swim experience with the sea lions. Watch the video →

Crew & navigation equipment

An experienced crew and sophisticated onboard equipment: GPS, radar, echo sounder, radio beacon, life rafts and the relevant pyrotechnics (signal and smoke flares).

On board

We give every passenger a free seasickness tablet at the start of the trip. Following our recommendations, getting seasick is unlikely.

Travel insurance

All passengers are insured throughout the voyage.

Weather policy

For your safety, the tour may be rescheduled or cancelled when sea or weather conditions require it; in that case we'll arrange a new date or the appropriate option with you.
