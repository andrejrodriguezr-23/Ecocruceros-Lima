Our story

30 years on the open sea

We are Peru's first marine ecotourism operator: creators, drivers, promoters and defenders of the Palomino Islands — Callao. Over the years, this effort has let us witness the recovery of the ecosystem and of its seabird and sea-lion populations.

Ecocruceros was born in La Punta, Callao, with a mission to bring people closer to Pacific marine wildlife — responsibly, safely and memorably.

What defines us

Three pillars of our voyage

Pioneers in marine ecotourism

Peru's first marine ecotourism operator. As creators and defenders of the Palomino Islands, we've watched the ecosystem and its seabird and sea-lion populations recover.

Experience & conservation

Our crew and collaborators are long-standing sailors — the most experienced and knowledgeable of the Palomino Islands circuit and the Callao islands area.

Safety & comfort

The yacht is the vessel that best suits our passengers. We carry sophisticated equipment: radar, echo sounder, radio beacon, GPS, life vests and life rafts.
