Marine wildlife

The wildlife of the Palomino Islands

Seabirds, sea lions and other species of Peru's cold sea that you'll see on the voyage. Tap a card to see its behavior, diet, habitat and breeding.

Bird

Humboldt Penguin

Pingüino de Humboldt · Spheniscus humboldti

Lives ~20 yrs

Behavior

Monogamous; they fish in small groups and nest in large colonies. They can dive to depths of up to 80 m.

Diet

  • Anchovy
  • Silverside
  • Sardines

Habitat

Islands and rocky points of Peru and northern Chile.

Breeding

Forms colonies to nest and nurseries to protect the chicks.

Bird

Guanay cormorant

Guanay · Phalacrocorax bougainvillii

Lives ~20 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and forms colonies to nest. It produces the highest-quality guano (about 50 g).

Diet

  • Anchovy
  • Silverside
  • Sardines
  • Squid

Habitat

Rocky coasts and islands.

Breeding

Nests on the flat ground of the islands, forming craters of guano and algae.

Bird

Red-legged cormorant

Chuita o Cormorán de pata roja · Phalacrocorax gaimardi

Lives ~25 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and forms colonies to nest. It can dive by propelling itself with its legs.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Crustaceans
  • Small amphibians

Habitat

High rocky cliffs.

Breeding

Nests near the tide line, between August and October.

Bird

Neotropic cormorant

Cormorán neotropical · Phalacrocorax brasilianus

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and forms colonies always near water. It grunts like a pig and flaps over the water to attract fish.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Crustaceans
  • Small amphibians

Habitat

Coasts, islands, beaches and freshwater bodies up to 4,500 m above sea level.

Breeding

Nests in trees or on island ground. They are monogamous and build their nests with branches.

Bird

Peruvian Pelican

Pelícano peruano · Pelecanus thagus

Lives ~25 yrs

Behavior

Fishes in small groups. It has a gular sac under its beak that lets it fish by draining water like a net.

Diet

  • Horse mackerel
  • Anchovy
  • Sardines

Habitat

Islands and coasts.

Breeding

Forms colonies to nest on island cliffs. During this season, the sac under its beak becomes brighter.

Bird

Inca Tern

Zarcillo · Larosterna inca

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Sociable; lives in flocks of up to 1,000 individuals. Considered the most beautiful gull in Peru.

Diet

  • Anchovy
  • Crustaceans
  • Silverside

Habitat

Islands and coasts. Native to the Peruvian Current.

Breeding

Chooses cracks and rock cliffs. Its season is all year round, mainly in April, May, October and November.

Bird

Band-tailed Gull

Gaviota peruana · Larus belcheri

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and lives in large colonies. It is a kleptoparasitic bird — it steals food from other birds.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Amphibians
  • Mollusks
  • Eggs
  • Chicks
  • Carrion

Habitat

Coastal beaches, on rocks or islets, and in low areas at water level.

Breeding

They are aggressive during nesting season.

Bird

Kelp Gull

Gaviota dominicana · Larus dominicanus

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and monogamous. Recognized as the largest gull in South America.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Amphibians
  • Mollusks
  • Eggs
  • Chicks
  • Carrion

Habitat

Beaches and rocky areas.

Breeding

Nests on coasts, islands and rivers.

Bird

Elegant Tern

Gaviotín elegante · Thalasseus elegans

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Migratory coastal birds that arrive on Peru's coast during their non-breeding season.

Diet

  • Anchovies
  • Sardines

Habitat

Coasts, beaches and estuaries.

Breeding

Nests in colonies and builds its nests on the beach floor.

Bird

Peruvian Booby

Piquero peruano · Sula variegata

Lives ~17 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and forms colonies to nest. It dives and can plunge to depths of up to 10 m.

Diet

  • Anchovy
  • Silverside
  • Sardine

Habitat

Offshore islands.

Breeding

Forms colonies to nest and sometimes builds no nest at all.

Bird

Blackish Oystercatcher

Ostrero negro suramericano · Haematopus ater

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Usually found alone or in pairs (monogamous). Uncommon, but can be found in small flocks.

Diet

  • Mussels
  • Mole crabs
  • Worms
  • Crustaceans

Habitat

Coasts, grasslands and marshes.

Breeding

Its breeding season is between September and December.

Bird

Surf Cinclodes

Churrete marisquero · Cinclodes taczanowskii

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Lives in small groups, hopping among the rocks where the waves break.

Diet

  • Snails
  • Marine plants
  • Invertebrates

Habitat

Arid rocky coasts.

Breeding

Its season is between October and January. It builds its nests in rocky ravines.

Bird

Black Vulture

Gallinazo de cabeza negra · Coragyps atratus

Lives ~20 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and forms colonies. It has a well-developed sense of smell to locate its food.

Diet

  • Carrion
  • Refuse
  • Mammals

Habitat

Coasts, grasslands and marshes.

Breeding

Nests in hollow trees, caves and on the ground. Its season is November and December.

Bird

Turkey Vulture

Gallinazo de cabeza roja · Cathartes aura

Lives ~20 yrs

Behavior

Solitary by day, but gathers at night. It is rare to see it in populated areas.

Diet

  • Carrion
  • Seeds
  • Leaves

Habitat

In Peru it is common on the coast, in the highlands and in the jungle.

Breeding

Nests in hollow trees, caves and on the ground. Its season is November and December.

Bird

Snowy Egret

Garcita blanca · Egretta thula

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Sociable; it gathers in flocks to roost in trees. It makes circular movements in the water to find food.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Invertebrates
  • Amphibians

Habitat

Freshwater and saltwater bodies, marshes, beaches and lagoons.

Breeding

Its season is November and December. Both parents incubate for 24 days.

Bird

Grey Gull

Gaviota gris · Leucophaeus modestus

Lives ~20 yrs

Behavior

Sociable; forms colonies to nest. It stands on the shore and dips its beak to catch mole crabs.

Diet

  • Anchovy
  • Small crustaceans

Habitat

Coasts and islands of South America.

Breeding

Nests on sand in desert areas, from January to March.

Bird

White-vented storm petrel

Golondrina de mar chica · Oceanites gracilis

Lives ~7 yrs

Behavior

It is solitary and nests in small scattered groups. Its ability to walk is limited.

Diet

  • Invertebrates
  • Fish

Habitat

Open sea, in the Humboldt Current.

Breeding

Nests in underground cavities or rock crevices throughout the year.

Bird

Peregrine Falcon

Halcón peregrino · Falco peregrinus

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Territorial. It flies at over 100 km/h — one of the fastest animals on the planet.

Diet

  • Birds
  • Mammals
  • Reptiles
  • Insects

Habitat

Coasts and cliffs.

Breeding

It usually nests on cliffs. Incubation lasts 33 days.

Bird

Grey Petrel

Petrel gris · Procellaria cinerea

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

It associates with cetaceans to take advantage of their prey's remains. It seems to run on the water to gain momentum for flight.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Shrimp
  • Octopus

Habitat

Very rare on the coast; it can be found along the Peruvian shore.

Breeding

It breeds in colonies and nests in dry places. Its season is between September and April.

Bird

Blue-footed Booby

Piquero de patas azules · Sula nebouxii

Lives ~17 yrs

Behavior

Sociable and forms colonies to nest. Unlike other birds, it breathes through its mouth as an adaptation to diving.

Diet

  • Anchovy
  • Mackerel
  • Sardine
  • Squid

Habitat

Coasts and islands.

Breeding

Chooses flat parts of islands and uses its feet to warm the eggs.

Bird

Ruddy Turnstone

Vuelvepiedras rojizo · Arenaria interpres

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Solitary, but associates in small groups. It is migratory, arriving in Peru in September and April.

Diet

  • Mollusks
  • Crustaceans
  • Small insects

Habitat

Sandy and rocky sea shores.

Breeding

Its breeding season is between May and June; incubation lasts 21 days.

Bird

Whimbrel

Zarapito trinador · Numenius phaeopus

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

Solitary; it usually forms small flocks with other birds. To feed, it walks along the shore plunging its beak.

Diet

  • Worms
  • Mollusks
  • Berries
  • Small insects

Habitat

Migratory; coasts and sea shores around the world.

Breeding

It breeds year-round. The young leave the nest at birth.

Mammal

Common Dolphin

Delfín común · Delphinus delphis

Lives ~40 yrs

Behavior

Lives in groups of up to 12 individuals. It uses echolocation through sound waves to find its food.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Mollusks
  • Crustaceans

Habitat

Open sea and coastal areas.

Breeding

They are polygamous; the gestation period is 12 months.

Mammal

South American fur seal

Lobo fino · Arctocephalus australis

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

It travels in groups of 15 to 20 individuals, although it also moves around alone.

Diet

  • Crustaceans
  • Mollusks
  • Birds
  • Fish

Habitat

Rocky coasts; it usually seeks some kind of shade.

Breeding

Its gestation period is 18 months.

Mammal

South American Sea Lion

Lobo chusco (lobo marino) · Otaria flavescens

Lives ~15 yrs

Behavior

It is social. It lives in colonies during the breeding and rearing seasons.

Diet

  • Fish
  • Crustaceans
  • Mollusks
  • Birds

Habitat

Rocky areas with crevices or caves.

Breeding

They gather in rookeries, forming a harem of one male and 15 females. Gestation lasts 8 months.
